\begin{abstract}
	With the proliferation of Large Language Models (LLMs) in educational technology, the challenges of operational cost, latency, and contextual relevance have become critical barriers to scaling personalized mentorship. This project introduces \textbf{Origin AI}, a context-aware, multimodal AI mentor designed for high-stakes competitive examination preparation. Origin AI implements a novel \textbf{4-Layer Context Assembly} model that integrates student history, real-time UI state, visual highlights, and multimodal queries to provide in-situ assistance without manual context description. To address financial sustainability, we propose a \textbf{Tiered Orchestration Pipeline} that prioritizes zero-cost local assets (stored solutions and subject-specific knowledge bases) before escalating to synthetic LLM generation. Experimental results demonstrate an 85\% reduction in token consumption and a 73\% reduction in median latency, while achieving 96\% subject resolution accuracy via a scoring-based \textbf{Cross-Subject Gate}. The system also incorporates pedagogical guardrails to enforce academic integrity and hint-first mentorship, ensuring that the AI functions as a teacher rather than a solution-providing proxy.
\end{abstract}
